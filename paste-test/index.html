Do Now paste test

Each button puts the quiz on the clipboard in a different format. After each one, switch to PowerPoint, click an empty slide, press Ctrl+V and note what appears. The questions are invented samples.

Answers slide (no cover: that stays in your deck)

What to check

  • A / D (picture): does it paste as one sharp picture filling the slide? (Drag a corner to fit if not.)
  • B / E (table): does it paste as a real PowerPoint table? Are the green colours, bold, line breaks and the Consolas code kept? Can you edit the text?
  • C (SVG): does anything paste? If a picture appears, right-click it: is there Convert to Shape, and does that give editable text?
  • Cover test: on a slide, draw a dark green rectangle where the answers go. Paste D, then right-click the picture → Send to Back. Is the cover still on top and draggable in the slideshow?
  • Note whether you used PowerPoint desktop or PowerPoint in the browser, and which browser.
