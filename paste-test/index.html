Do Now paste test

Each button puts the quiz on the clipboard in a different format. After each one, switch to PowerPoint, click an empty slide, press Ctrl+V and note what appears. The questions are invented samples.

What to check

  • A / D (picture): does it paste as one sharp picture filling the slide? (Drag a corner to fit if not.)
  • B / E (table): does it paste as a real PowerPoint table? Are the green colours, bold, line breaks and the Consolas code kept? Can you edit the text?
  • C (SVG): does anything paste? If a picture appears, right-click it: is there Convert to Shape, and does that give editable text?
  • F (new): paste, then Convert to Shape. Is each question one text box now (not one per line)? Type extra words into question 1: does the text wrap onto a new line inside the card? Are the bold words and the code still right?
  • G (new): paste, Convert to Shape, then press Ctrl+Shift+G once to ungroup. Does the dark green cover sit on top of the answers? Can you drag the cover with its words as one piece? Does the selection pane (Home → Arrange → Selection Pane) call it "Answer cover (drag to reveal)"?
  • Icon: is the orange Individual work figure there after Convert to Shape, or does it disappear or go blurry?
  • Old cover test (picture): on a slide, draw a dark green rectangle where the answers go. Paste D, then right-click the picture → Send to Back. Is the cover still on top and draggable in the slideshow?
  • Note whether you used PowerPoint desktop or PowerPoint in the browser, and which browser.
